LibraScan Download

Borrow library books by scanning them

LibraScan is the Pampanga State University library app. Scan the QR code on a book, wait for the librarian to approve your request, then pick the book up at the desk.

Download for Android

How borrowing works

  1. Scan the book

    Open the scanner, choose Borrow, and point your camera at the QR code on the book. If the camera can't read it, upload a photo of the code instead.

  2. Wait for approval

    Your request goes to the library. You get a notification when a librarian approves or declines it.

  3. Pick it up

    Once approved, collect the book at the library desk.

  4. Return it

    Scan the same code again with the scanner set to Return. The app shows your due dates so you know when.

What else is in the app

Catalog and search

Browse the books on the shelves, or search online books.

My books

See each book you've borrowed and whether it's returned, due soon, or overdue.

AI librarian

Ask what to read next or how to find books on a topic, and get suggestions from the catalog.

Notifications

Your phone tells you when a librarian answers a borrow request.
